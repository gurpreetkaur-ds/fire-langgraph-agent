# Northwind Labs — Remote Work Policy (v3.2)

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period may work remotely up to three days per week. Contractors need written approval from their engagement manager.

## Core hours
Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 Central European Time. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without consent.

## Equipment and security
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office stipend of 450 EUR. All remote access to internal systems must go through the corporate VPN (WireGuard). Using public Wi-Fi without the VPN is a policy violation. Lost devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within 4 hours.

## Expenses
Internet costs are reimbursed up to 30 EUR per month with an invoice. Co-working spaces are reimbursed up to 200 EUR per month when the employee lives more than 50 km from an office.

## Working abroad
Employees may work from another EU country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Working from outside the EU requires HR and Legal approval at least 30 days in advance.
